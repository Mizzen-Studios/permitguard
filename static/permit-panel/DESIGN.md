# Permit panel design

The panel borrows the organisation of a physical isolation permit: a blue clipboard spine, a pale equipment sheet, and individually tagged personal locks. The lock rail, not a dashboard of metrics, is the memorable visual.

Tokens: clipboard blue `#204c75`, paper `#f7fafc`, ink `#183246`, line `#bdcbd5`, warning amber `#f2bd51`, stop red `#a42d3b`. Local system humanist sans for readable instructions; compact tabular numbers for equipment tags. No downloaded fonts or images.

Layout: left-aligned equipment identity and actual sequential step spine above a wide working sheet; attached right-hand lock rail on tablets, below on phones. Footer holds one primary action; supporting evidence actions remain secondary. No decorative metrics, gradients, or marketing copy.

Review: rejected black-and-neon factory dashboard and generic card grid. The physical permit sheet + lock rail better explains whose personal lock blocks release. Native controls, large targets, visible focus, reduced-motion safe, textual status alongside colour.

This is local browser demo state, not authorization or enforcement. Forge integration must replace demo actions with server-owned transitions and identity. No camera/QR/Realtime capability is claimed as live-tested.
