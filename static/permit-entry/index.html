Open the permit panel

  1. Open this Jira issue.
  2. Click the apps icon under the issue title (it may be labelled “View app actions”).
  3. Choose PermitGuard.

This is a navigation guide, not a live readiness result. No permit is created or changed here. The full panel shows authenticated state and its version/build marker. Follow your site procedure; PermitGuard is decision support only.
