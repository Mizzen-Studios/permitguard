PermitGuard Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: 27 November 2026

PermitGuard is a Forge-based lockout/tagout (LOTO) decision-support evaluation app provided by Mizzen Studios. Contact: ollymcbride4@gmail.com. This policy describes the app's current data handling, not a claim of safety certification or privacy-compliance certification.

Important current limits: the app stores personal data in Atlassian Forge. There is no complete automatic erase, retention or personal-data reporting/refresh flow yet. Use synthetic evaluation content; do not upload real plant, confidential workforce, sensitive personal or unnecessary identifying information. This policy does not remove those implementation gaps.

Data the app processes

  • Atlassian account IDs and, when available, display names of signed-in users, workers and assigned verifiers.
  • Jira issue/project identifiers, crew and equipment/isolation-point information, permit states, attributable lock records, verification/activation requests, attestations, audit events and timestamps.
  • Photos explicitly submitted as evidence: synthetic demo evidence only for the hackathon. Do not upload real plant or workforce photographs, faces, badges or identifying site details. Image bytes, metadata and free text may contain personal or location information.
  • Configuration, permission checks and short-lived QR challenge credentials. Expiration of a challenge's validity does not promise immediate deletion of its stored record.

Purposes and access

Data supports displaying permit state, attributing each person's actions, checking shared-equipment lock blockers, assigning verification, protecting updates against stale writes and providing authorized readiness responses. Normal access uses Atlassian authentication and the caller's permissions, with additional app checks. Shared-lock summaries may disclose an aggregate blocker without another issue's identities or lock details. No submitted evidence, QR scan or app response proves physical isolation or presence.

Storage and sharing

Operational app records and submitted evidence are stored in Atlassian-hosted Forge Custom Entity Store/KVS, scoped to the installation/environment. Derived state can be stored in Jira issue properties. Optional Confluence archive code can write a closed summary to an explicitly selected, administrator-approved restricted space; this is not configured in the current Jira-only rehearsal.

The app has no vendor-operated external storage, external runtime egress, custom analytics, advertising trackers or external AI API configured. This is not a promise that no data can ever leave Atlassian: authorized users receive data in their browsers and can copy/export it. If an administrator later approves an external MCP client, that client can receive authorized tool results and its own privacy/storage rules apply. MCP exposure is off for the current configuration. The declared MCP surface is a single read-only readiness tool; platform consent may be broader than that tool's behavior.

Optional Rovo and platform providers

Optional native Atlassian Rovo can use authorized app responses for readiness or a PENDING proposal. It cannot complete confirmation or activation through the app's declared agent tools. Rovo and Atlassian services are governed by their applicable service terms, privacy policies and subprocessors. We do not promise a particular model-training or provider-retention setting. PermitGuard does not use the Forge LLM API or a separately operated external AI service.

Retention, correction and deletion

Default retention is administrator-controlled, with no automatic expiry. Closed permits, audit records, synthetic demo photos and archive copies are retained in the customer's Atlassian site until the site administrator arranges their deletion or uninstalls the app. Operational app data stays in Atlassian-hosted Forge storage and Jira; optional archive copies, if configured, stay in the customer's Confluence space. Mizzen Studios does not maintain vendor copies of operational app records.

Uninstalling the app puts Forge-hosted storage through Atlassian's standard data retention and deletion lifecycle, including its soft-deletion and recovery periods; it does not mean immediate permanent erasure. Jira properties and any Confluence archive copies may need separate site-administrator cleanup and are not promised to disappear on uninstall.

There is no automatic deletion or anonymisation. The app provides no complete self-service administrative erase tool, automatic personal-data reporting/refresh process or deletion-time guarantee. Privacy groundwork remains inactive. An erasure request, retention policy or account disappearance never authorizes changing active personal-lock ownership, recording a lock as removed, or clearing a permit. Required safety, audit and privacy obligations need human reconciliation; this is not an exemption from applicable rights.

Erasure requests are human-reviewed by the customer's Atlassian site administrator, in their own site. Mizzen Studios support at ollymcbride4@gmail.com only routes requests and holds no copies of the requested operational records. Support correspondence itself is handled by the email provider as described below. Do not send passwords, tokens, evidence photos, permit exports or workforce records to support. Contact your site administrator for access, correction or deletion; they must assess identity, authority and applicable obligations. No support response/deletion deadline is promised here.

Atlassian's personal-data reporting/erase/refresh requirements remain an implementation gap; Forge storage and this agreed retention policy do not establish compliance. No new privacy tooling or scheduled processing is enabled by this policy.

Logs, this website and contacting us

The app aims to keep diagnostic logs minimal (operation/error codes rather than photos or permit content); Atlassian may process platform/service logs under its own policies. These static legal pages are hosted on GitHub Pages without app-added analytics, scripts, forms or tracking cookies. GitHub may process visitors' IP addresses and request metadata under GitHub's privacy statement. Support emails are handled by the email provider outside Forge; the no-external-app-storage statement does not mean website hosting or email uses Atlassian storage.

Changes

We may update this policy as the implementation changes and will update the date on this page. Any new external integration, material data use or permission requirement must be reviewed separately; this page does not itself enable an integration. See also the evaluation terms.
